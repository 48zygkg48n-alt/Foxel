Questions

Do you need my Roblox password?

No. Foxel never needs your password, cookies or account access. We only need an accessible gamepass link or your Roblox username.

How much Robux will I receive?

The amount you enter is the gamepass price. Roblox applies its standard 30% gamepass fee, so you receive 70% of that amount. For example, a 5,000 Robux gamepass results in 3,500 Robux after the fee.

How long does delivery take?

A team member usually purchases the gamepass within 24 hours, depending on when the team is online. After the purchase, Roblox may hold the Robux as pending for up to 5 days before they become available.

What if my gamepass link is wrong?

Contact the team through our Telegram support bot so we can help check it before the order proceeds.

What if Foxel can’t complete my order?

If we cannot fulfil your order, we return the funds Foxel receives for it. Fees charged and kept by an external payment provider or crypto network may not be recoverable.

Are there extra payment fees?

Depending on the payment method, extra fees may apply. Card payments can include a 5% fee, while crypto networks may charge their own transaction or gas fees. Check the final amount shown before paying.

How can the team contact me?

Provide either an email address or a Telegram username. You only need one of these contact methods.

Can I order a custom amount?

Yes. Custom orders start at 5,000 Robux, and larger orders receive better rates. A 1,000 Robux preset is also available.

How do I contact support?

Use the Foxel support bot to open a ticket. The team replies when members are online, on Amsterdam time.
